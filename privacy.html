Backplay Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Backplay does not collect or transmit personal data, browsing history, or video and audio activity. No account is required. Backplay contains no advertising trackers or analytics.

Data on your device

Your list of configured websites, PiP button placement, preferences, and purchase status are stored on your device. The app and its Safari extension share settings through an App Group. This information is not sent to the developer.

Website access

The Safari extension needs website access to keep media playing and add your configured Picture in Picture button on pages with video. It processes pages locally and does not send their contents to the developer. Websites you visit remain subject to their own privacy policies.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases and restores through the App Store. Backplay uses StoreKit to check whether the one-time unlock was purchased. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Contact

If you email support, we use the information you choose to send to respond to your request. Contact aikb2b@gmail.com.
