Backplay Support

For questions, feedback, or bug reports, email aikb2b@gmail.com. Please include your iOS version, the affected website, and steps to reproduce the issue. Avoid sending private browsing information.

Enable the extension

Open Settings > Apps > Safari > Extensions > Backplay. Turn on Backplay and set website access to Allow for the pages where you want to use it.

Add a Picture in Picture button

Open a page with video in Safari, open Backplay from Safari’s extensions menu, and choose “Add PiP Button to Player.” Select a control in the player, adjust the placement, and save.

Manage websites

Use the Sites tab in Backplay to enable, disable, or remove saved sites. Changes are applied when the extension next synchronizes.

Purchases and restores

Background playback is free. You can configure a PiP button on one website for free; a one-time purchase unlocks additional websites. Use Restore Purchases on the unlock screen with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

Apple handles billing and refund requests. For a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
